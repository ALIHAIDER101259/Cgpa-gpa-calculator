# 🎓 GradeFlow — Smart GPA & CGPA Calculator

**A modern, interactive, and student-friendly academic performance dashboard built with HTML, CSS, and Vanilla JavaScript.**

GradeFlow helps students calculate their semester GPA, track their cumulative CGPA, analyze academic performance, and plan their future grades through a clean and responsive interface.

## ✨ Features

### 📊 GPA & CGPA Calculation
- Calculate GPA using course grades and credit hours.
- Calculate cumulative CGPA across multiple semesters.
- Add, edit, and remove subjects.
- Automatically calculate total credit hours and grade points.
- Validate inputs to prevent incorrect calculations.

### 🎯 Smart Target CGPA Planner
- Set your desired target CGPA.
- Calculate the GPA required in upcoming semesters.
- Estimate the academic performance needed to reach your goal.
- Understand how future grades can affect your cumulative CGPA.

### 📈 Academic Analytics
- View semester-wise GPA and cumulative CGPA.
- Track academic progress over time.
- Display performance summaries and key statistics.
- Visualize grade distribution and academic trends.

### 📚 Semester Management
- Organize academic records by semester.
- Review previous semester results.
- Keep track of completed credit hours.
- Compare performance between semesters.

### 🎨 Modern User Interface
- Attractive dashboard with a clean layout.
- Responsive design for desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- Interactive cards and visual statistics.
- Modern typography, spacing, and color combinations.
- Smooth transitions and intuitive navigation.

### ⚡ User Experience
- Instant calculations when grades or credit hours change.
- Easy-to-use subject management.
- Clear result summaries.
- Helpful validation messages and error handling.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- **HTML5** — Website structure
- **CSS3** — Styling, layouts, animations, and responsive design
- **Vanilla JavaScript** — Calculations, validation, and interactivity

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open the project folder.
3. Open `index.html` in your browser.
4. Enter your subjects, grades, and credit hours.
5. Calculate your GPA or CGPA and explore the available features.

## 🎯 Project Goal

The goal of GradeFlow is to make academic performance tracking simple, visual, and useful for university students. Instead of calculating grades manually, students can organize their academic information and make informed decisions about their future semesters.
